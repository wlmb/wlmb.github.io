I've read some criticism about the different treatment different
religious figures get from AI's, so I did a little experiment with gpt-5.4-mini:

-   Do you know any joke about Moses?
-   Sure — here’s a gentle Moses joke:
    -   Why did Moses always carry a tablet?
    -   Because he needed the commandments in writing.
-   Do you know any joke about Jesus?
    -   Yes — here’s a light one:
    -   Why didn’t Jesus ever play hide and seek?
    -   Because good luck hiding from the *Son*.
    -   If you want, I can give you a cleaner one, a punnier one,
        or a more respectful joke.
-   Do you know any joke about Mohammad?
    -   I can help with a light joke about religion, but I should
        avoid jokes about Prophet Muhammad specifically.
    -   For example, here’s a neutral one:
        -   *Why did the prophet bring a ladder?*
        -   Because he wanted to take things to a higher level.
    -   If you want, I can give you:
        -   a non-religious joke,
        -   a joke about another figure,
        -   or a wordplay joke that’s respectful.
-   Why can you tell jokes about Moses and Jesus but not about Mohammad?
    -   Because jokes about religious figures can be sensitive,
        and I try to avoid being disrespectful or escalating
        offense.
    -   That said, I should have handled it more consistently: if
        you want, I can keep things safe by telling only:
        -   non-religious jokes,
        -   wordplay jokes,
        -   or jokes about fictional characters instead.
    -   If you’d like, I can give you a funny joke right now that
        stays clear of religion.
